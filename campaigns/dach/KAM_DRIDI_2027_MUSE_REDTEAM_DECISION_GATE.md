## Decision hierarchy
- **First:** written genre-fit licensing offer with meaningful financing, transparent rights, real retail execution, and reporting.
- **Second:** direct physical distribution with demonstrable retailer access, low fixed cost, and no mandatory expensive promo.
- **Third:** demand-led direct/preorder pressing only after verified demand and an acceptable cash-at-risk limit.
- **No deal:** digital independent release plus tour merchandise, with physical launch postponed.
- **7Hard:** benchmark/negotiation fallback, never automatically selected; EUR 750 single and EUR 2,950 album promo are different scopes. Compare actual deliverables.

## Physical economics: model three routes before signing
Use a single comparable EUR / net-of-VAT model with:
- pressing quantity, manufacturing per unit, setup, inbound freight;
- wholesale PPD, distributor percentage, storage, handling, returns and sell-through;
- direct price, platform fee, fulfilment, preorder count;
- artist-financed marketing and fixed costs;
- licensing advance, recoupment basis, royalty percentage, rights/territory/term;
- downside/base/upside sales scenarios and maximum upfront cash at risk.

**Illustrative calculation, not real quotes:** At 300 units pressed and 60% sell-through of 200 shipped units, an artist-financed distributor scenario can lose EUR 288; a 150-unit direct preorder scenario with 100 paid orders can contribute EUR 350; a partner-financed license can contribute EUR 288 with no manufacturing cash outlay. This proves that the preferred route depends on actual economics and risk appetite, not a slogan.

## GO / NO-GO contract gates
| Gate | Minimum evidence | Hard stop |
|---|---|---|
| Rights | Territory, format, exclusivity, master ownership in writing | Blanket future-catalogue lock |
| Retail | Named wholesale routes, stock intake and servicing | Merely 'available to order' |
| Economics | PPD, all commissions, manufacturing, returns, deductions | Mandatory opaque PR package |
| Reporting | Sales and stock reports, payment schedule, audit rights | No written reporting |
| Marketing | Specific work, channels, schedule, reporting, proof | Unverifiable exposure promises |
| Duration | Defined term, end-of-term stock disposition, no silent renewal | Indefinite lock or automatic extension without control |
| Cash | Maximum committed artist spend and downside case | Unapproved spending |
| Release | Approved product and date | Proposed date treated as confirmed |
