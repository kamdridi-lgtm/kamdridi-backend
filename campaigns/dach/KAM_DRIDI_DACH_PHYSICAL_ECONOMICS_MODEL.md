## Route A1 — label-financed
Artist upfront cash:
0 preferred

Basic contribution model:
Artist receipts = max(0, Net label receipts - recoupable costs) × artist share
plus any non-recoupable advance if applicable.

Questions that must be answered:
- Is royalty based on PPD, net receipts, or "all income"?
- Which costs are recoupable?
- Are returns charged against artist share?
- Is marketing recoupable?
- Is manufacturing recoupable?
- Is there an advance?
- What happens to unsold stock at termination?

## Route A2 — direct distributor
If KAM DRIDI / a third party supplies stock:

Gross wholesale receipts = Units sold × P
Distribution fee = Gross wholesale receipts × D
Variable handling = Units sold × H
Return cost = Units returned × R
Storage = months × W
Net distribution receipts = Gross wholesale receipts - distribution fee - handling - returns - storage

If pressing is artist-funded:
Cash exposure = Q × M + S + F + approved MK

If pressing is partner/demand-funded:
Cash exposure can remain near zero.

## Route B — demand-funded micro-run
Preorder revenue = PO × RP
Payment fees = preorder revenue × PF
Direct fulfilment = PO × FF
Pressing cost = Q × M + S + F
Net preorder contribution = preorder revenue - payment fees - fulfilment - pressing cost

GO condition:
preorder / PO cash covers manufacturing and fulfilment before order placement, unless Karim separately approves a cash contribution.

## Route C — POD
Net per order = RP - POD unit cost - payment fee - fulfilment/shipping subsidy
No inventory risk.
Not a wholesale-retail strategy.

## Decision rule
No route is selected until the model can show:
1. expected contribution;
2. maximum artist cash exposure;
3. rights cost;
4. measurable DACH retail reach;
5. operational burden;
6. downside if sell-through is weak.

The best route is the one with the strongest combination of real retail reach, clean rights, low cash exposure and transparent economics.
