# KAM DRIDI — IDEAL DACH PHYSICAL DISTRIBUTION / LICENSING TERM SHEET
Prepared: 2026-10-20
Status: INTERNAL NEGOTIATION BASELINE — NOT A CONTRACT

## Objective
Secure real Germany / Austria / Switzerland physical distribution for:
- TWICE UPON A TIME — new 2027 album
- ECHOES UNEARTHED — Album #1 / back catalogue

without unnecessary rights transfer, mandatory high-cost promotion, or speculative artist-funded inventory.

## 1. Territory
Preferred: Germany, Austria, Switzerland only.
Expansion outside DACH requires separate written approval and measurable additional value.

## 2. Rights
Preferred direct-distribution route:
- physical distribution rights only;
- no publishing rights;
- no ownership transfer of masters;
- no future-catalogue option;
- digital rights remain with KAM DRIDI unless separately negotiated.

Preferred licensing route:
- limited master-use licence by product, format, territory and term;
- KAM DRIDI retains master ownership.

## 3. Term
Target: 12–24 months for distribution; up to 36 months only if the partner materially finances stock / marketing / retail execution.
No uncontrolled evergreen renewal.
Artist termination right with reasonable notice.

## 4. Manufacturing
Preferred order:
1. partner-financed first pressing;
2. demand-funded pressing via verified preorders / retailer POs;
3. artist-financed pressing only by Karim's separate explicit approval.

No manufacturing charge may be imposed without written quote and approval.

## 5. Initial inventory
Quantity must be evidence-based.
No speculative 500–1,000-unit requirement merely to obtain a listing.
Partner must state:
- initial intake;
- warehouse location;
- reorder threshold;
- reorder lead time;
- minimum repress quantity.

## 6. Retail execution
Partner must state whether it performs:
- retailer solicitation;
- wholesale account servicing;
- mail-order servicing;
- catalogue pitching;
- replenishment;
- returns handling;
- stock reporting.

"Orderable" alone is insufficient.

## 7. Economics
All of the following must be explicit:
- PPD / wholesale base;
- distributor commission;
- label share / royalty if licensing;
- manufacturing recoupment if any;
- storage;
- pick / pack;
- return fees;
- deductions;
- payment timing;
- currency;
- VAT treatment.

No undefined category such as "third party 50%" may remain in final agreement.

## 8. Promotion
No mandatory €2,950-style campaign as a condition of physical distribution.
Any paid promo must be optional, separately scoped, measurable and separately approved.
No unverifiable chart or traffic guarantees.

## 9. Reporting
Minimum quarterly:
- units shipped;
- units sold;
- units returned;
- closing inventory;
- net receipts;
- deductions;
- territory / account detail where available.

KAM DRIDI should have reasonable audit / statement-query rights.

## 10. Returns / obsolete stock
Agreement must state:
- who bears returns;
- return fee;
- write-off rules;
- end-of-term stock disposition;
- option for KAM DRIDI to purchase or recover remaining stock at defined cost.

## 11. Back catalogue
ECHOES UNEARTHED must be clearly identified as Album #1 / back catalogue.
No rebranding as a new album.
TWICE UPON A TIME remains Album #2 / active campaign product.

## 12. Exclusivity
Exclusivity, if any, must be:
- territory-limited;
- format-limited;
- product-specific;
- term-limited;
- tied to performance obligations.

## 13. Performance / exit trigger
KAM DRIDI should be able to terminate or convert exclusivity if the partner fails to:
- onboard stock by agreed deadline;
- report;
- pay;
- service agreed retail channels;
- maintain product availability.

## 14. No-go terms
- world rights by default;
- future catalogue option;
- publishing grab;
- perpetual master rights;
- mandatory promo spend;
- silent annual renewal;
- undefined deductions;
- no reporting;
- no stock accountability;
- rights without measurable partner obligations.

## Negotiation line
KAM DRIDI is open to either direct distribution or a financing/licensing structure. The winning route is the one that proves real retail execution, clean economics and limited rights.
