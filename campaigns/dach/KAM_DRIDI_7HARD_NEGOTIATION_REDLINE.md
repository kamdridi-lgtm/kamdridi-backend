## Objective
Keep 7Hard alive only if it beats the direct-distributor route on measurable value.

## Current offer / draft points under review
- Single package: EUR 750 + VAT
- Heavy promotion language: EUR 2,950 + VAT
- Physical route via ZYX referenced
- Draft economics: 70% CD PPD / 80% digital / 50% third-party
- Draft term: 3 years + renewal mechanics
- Returns: EUR 0.75 / unit
- Quarterly accounting

## Required corrections before GO

### 1. Heavy promotion must be optional
The EUR 2,950 Heavy package must not be mandatory as a condition of album distribution.
Any paid promotion must be separately scoped and separately approved.

### 2. Physical distribution must be defined
7Hard must confirm in writing:
- whether stock is physically warehoused;
- who manufactures;
- who pays manufacturing;
- initial intake quantity;
- named wholesale / retailer network;
- whether sales reps actively solicit accounts;
- mail-order servicing;
- reorder process;
- returns handling;
- stock reporting.

"Available through ZYX" alone is insufficient.

### 3. Economics must be unambiguous
Clarify:
- 70% of what exact PPD base?
- who sets PPD?
- what deductions occur before artist share?
- what does "third party 50%" mean?
- are manufacturing / marketing / storage recouped?
- are returns charged to artist share?
- payment timing and minimum payout.

### 4. Rights must be narrowed
Preferred:
- DACH territory only for physical;
- product-specific rights;
- no publishing rights;
- KAM DRIDI retains master ownership;
- no future-catalogue option;
- digital rights excluded unless 7Hard proves separate value.

### 5. Term / renewal
Preferred:
- 12–24 months distribution term;
- maximum 36 months only with meaningful partner investment;
- no silent automatic renewal;
- artist termination right with reasonable notice;
- clear end-of-term stock disposition.

### 6. Reporting
Quarterly is acceptable only if statements show:
- shipped;
- sold;
- returned;
- inventory;
- gross receipts;
- deductions;
- net artist amount.

### 7. Marketing proof
Any chart, Spotify, Amazon or YouTube promise must be:
- platform-compliant;
- genuine audience;
- reported with verifiable campaign evidence;
- not based on bought / incentivized / artificial traffic.

## GO conditions
7Hard becomes a serious GO candidate only if ALL are true:
1. Heavy promo is optional.
2. Real DACH physical retail servicing is documented.
3. Third-party 50% is defined.
4. Manufacturing/stock responsibility is explicit.
5. Rights/territory/term are narrowed.
6. Reporting and stock accounting are written.
7. No unapproved artist cash commitment.
8. Economics compare favorably with a direct-distributor route.

## NO-GO conditions
- Mandatory EUR 2,950 promotion
- Vague physical "availability" only
- Undefined deductions
- Broad multi-year rights without investment
- Future-catalogue lock
- No stock / sell-through reporting
- Artificial-traffic or unverifiable chart methodology

## Negotiation position
KAM DRIDI is not rejecting 7Hard categorically.
7Hard must earn the deal by proving measurable value over direct distribution.
