## Core correction
Real DACH physical retail can come through either:
- A1: label-financed licensing / P&D
- A2: direct physical distributor with real retailer servicing
- B: demand-funded micro-run / preorder / retailer PO
- C: POD / direct-to-fan floor

A route counts as real retail only if it has stock intake, retailer or wholesale servicing, replenishment, and reporting. Mere catalogue availability does not count.

## A1 — Label-financed licensing / P&D
GO only with written territory, format, economics, reporting, stock, returns, retail route, and partner-funded manufacturing.
Keep scope narrow, avoid unnecessary future-catalogue lock, and require a defined exit.

## A2 — Direct physical distributor
GO only if the distributor accepts stock and provides actual retailer servicing, not just orderability.
Require written stock intake, wholesale terms, warehouse/handling, returns, reorders, and reporting.

## B — Demand-funded micro-run
GO only if demand pre-funds stock through paid preorders, retailer POs, or a separately approved tour inventory need.
No speculative pressing.

## C — POD / direct-to-fan
Useful as a zero-stock fallback.
Do not treat this as proof of DACH retail distribution.
