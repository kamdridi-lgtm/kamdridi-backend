## Automatic GO threshold
A partner can move to GO only if:
1. score >=75/100;
2. no hard-stop clause;
3. real physical stock / wholesale servicing is documented;
4. no mandatory opaque promo fee;
5. rights, territory, term, reporting, returns and payment are written;
6. no unapproved artist cash commitment.

## Hard-stop clauses
- broad multi-year exclusivity without meaningful investment;
- mandatory expensive promotion as a condition of distribution;
- no stock or sell-through reporting;
- vague "chart guarantee" / traffic promises without methodology;
- undefined third-party deductions;
- no termination mechanism;
- passive orderability presented as active retail.
