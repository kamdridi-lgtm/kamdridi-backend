MONTRÉAL, CANADA — In a music business increasingly driven by follower counts and viral clips, KAM DRIDI is building an international story by another route: independent outreach, a cinematic melodic hard rock sound, and persistent contact with broadcasters and concert organizers.

His track OUR LOST DREAMS, from ECHOES UNEARTHED, has received documented attention across Australia, South Africa, Japan, the UK, Germany, the United States and Brazil. In Canberra, it reached No. 4 on Valley FM 89.5's own “Australia's Top 10” station chart — not a national chart. An outgoing 17 FOR EVER MP3 submission is recorded in Outlook on 17 October; a second airplay decision has not been confirmed.

A 2027 international live route is taking shape, with a Tokyo show at GEKIRIN / The Playhouse Machida on 17 January, a Wellington Fringe Bar performance on 2 March, Sydney Deadset on 5 March and Adelaide Fringe / Domain Theatre on 18 March. Other dates, including Cairns, must not be presented as confirmed without venue proof.

The unusual stage concept combines genuinely live lead vocals with professional instrumental backing tracks and optional synchronized cinematic stage video. KAM DRIDI does not travel as a full live band.

The story for editors: Can an independent rock artist build international reach without first becoming a social-media celebrity?
